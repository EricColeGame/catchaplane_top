## Step-by-Step: Catch a Plane How to Earn Money on Every Run

Here's the routine that experienced players follow, in order. Skipping steps is the fastest way to stall your income.

1. **Train strength before you touch a plane.** Grip training raises how long you can hold on, and hang time is the foundation of every payout. A few minutes of training beats ten failed grabs.
2. **Start light, then climb.** Your glove tier decides which planes you can actually grab. Chasing a heavy jet with starter gloves usually ends in a fall and zero income.
3. **Time your grab instead of spamming jump.** Wait for the plane to pass close, then jump once. Clean grabs save seconds, and seconds add up over a session.
4. **Bank your progress at the finish.** Reaching the finish is what converts a run into trophies and unlocks. A risky grab near the end can wipe out everything you built.
5. **Reinvest in gloves first.** Faster grabs and heavier planes compound. Cosmetic purchases do not.
6. **Open gear boxes only after your glove tier feels stable.** Otherwise you're spending on tools you can't use efficiently yet.
7. **Repeat with escalating aircraft.** Each cycle should end with a slightly better glove and a slightly bigger average payout.

| Session window | Focus | Goal before moving on |
|---|---|---|
| Minutes 0–5 | Strength training | Noticeably longer hang time |
| Minutes 5–15 | Light planes, clean grabs | Consistent income per run |
| Minutes 15–25 | First glove upgrade | Access to heavier planes |
| Minutes 25–30 | Finish-line runs | Trophies and a world unlock |

That's the short version of Catch a Plane how to earn money: build the grip, protect the run, and let upgrades do the heavy lifting.
