## How Plane Weight Works in Catch a Plane

Catch a Plane is an incremental simulator on Roblox from developer Oi_iO, built around a very simple joke: a friend overslept, missed his flight, and decided to sprint down the runway after it. The game's own description warns players not to try it in real life — and it's right, because in-game you're doing this at speed with a plane actively trying to throw you off.

Under the comedy, there are two separate progression stats, and confusing them is the number one reason players plateau:

| Stat | What it controls | How you raise it | What happens when it's too low |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grip strength** | How long you can stay attached once you've made contact | Training your strength between runs | You grab the plane, then slide off mid-climb |
| **Glove quality** | Which planes you can grab at all, and how fast you can grab them | Upgrading gloves | You whiff the grab entirely, or only latch onto light planes |

A player with huge strength but starter gloves can hang on forever — to planes that barely move. A player with great gloves but no strength sticks to a heavy jet for two seconds and drops. The **strongest plane** in Catch a Plane sits behind both stats at once, which is why the smartest players level them together rather than dumping everything into one.

## What Makes a Plane the "Strongest"?

| Trait | Why it matters | How to spot it |

## Step-by-Step: Landing the Catch a Plane Strongest Plane

Once your gloves are ready, the grab itself becomes a timing problem. Here's the routine that works consistently, whether you're on an early runway or a late-game world.

1. **Watch one full pass first.** Don't jump at the first plane you see. Track it from approach to exit so you know its speed and line.
2. **Position at the near edge of the grab zone.** Cutting the distance you have to cover makes fast planes far more catchable.
3. **Jump early, not late.** The window closes as the plane passes you — late jumps are the most common failed grab.
4. **Commit to the jump.** A hesitant hop wastes the pass and leaves you standing on the runway watching your ride leave.
5. **Hold steady through the initial drag.** The first second is the most violent; survive it and the ride smooths out.
6. **Deploy gear if you feel the slip.** A parachute or Gravity Wings turns a failed grab into a soft landing instead of a reset.

| Checkpoint | What to verify | Common failure |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the jump** | Gloves match the plane's weight class | Grabbing a plane two tiers above your gloves |
| **At the jump** | You left early, not late | Waiting for the plane to arrive |
| **During the drag** | You're holding, not fighting the camera | Over-steering into terrain |
| **Before landing** | Gear is ready to deploy | Panic-dropping and losing the run |
