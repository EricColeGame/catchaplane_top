## Power and Cash Are Separate Tracks, Not One Ladder

The catalog lists Power multipliers and Cash multipliers as separate product lines with separate price tags, and the gap between them is the most useful fact on the page. Power is cheap at the entry level. Cash is not. That asymmetry should shape every early decision in a **Catch a Plane fastest progression** plan.

| Tier | Power pass | Cash pass | Price difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| x2 | 6 Robux | 19 Robux | 13 Robux |
| x4 | 49 Robux | 69 Robux | 20 Robux |
| x6 | 99 Robux | 119 Robux | 20 Robux |

Read those numbers as published, not as a bundle. Each listing states its own multiplier, and the catalog does not say whether owning two tiers stacks, replaces the lower tier, or requires the cheaper tier first. Do not multiply 2 × 4 × 6 and assume a 48x result — nothing on the listing supports that math, and players who assume it tend to overspend.

Prices are a dated snapshot from October 24, 2026. Regional pricing, account-specific offers, and later updates can all change what you see, so the number on your confirmation screen is the only one that counts. If you want to compare against the live listing, check the game's page on the [official Roblox website](https://www.roblox.com) before you commit.

## Five Mistakes That Quietly Undo a Good Plan

| Mistake | Why it hurts | The fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assuming tiers stack | 2 × 4 × 6 is not documented anywhere | Buy one tier, test, then decide |
| Expecting upgrade-difference pricing | You pay the full listed price, not the gap | Budget the whole cost of the tier you want |
| Buying unnamed-effect passes first | No published numbers to compare against | Inspect live details before spending |
| Splitting a small budget across both resources | Two x2 passes cost 25, not 20 | Fund the bottleneck only |
| Ignoring the confirmation screen | Regional and account pricing can differ | Read the prompt before you confirm |

Before you confirm any purchase, run a short checklist:

1. Read the product name, the in-game explanation, and the purchase prompt together.
2. Check the price shown to your account, not a price you read somewhere else.
3. For Power versus Cash, match the resource to the goal you are chasing right now.
4. For any item or zone with no effect description, open its live details in game first.
5. If you cannot explain what a pass does in one sentence, wait.

That last rule alone will save most players more Robux than any tier comparison. A **Catch a Plane fastest progression** plan is not a shopping list — it is a filter for what you skip.

## FAQ

**Do I need to buy passes for Catch a Plane fastest progression?**
No. The creator's basic training and flight loop does not state a paid-pass requirement, so you can progress without spending. Passes accelerate the resource side of the game; they are not documented as a gate.

**Should I buy x2 Power or x2 Cash first?**
On price alone, x2 Power at 6 Robux is the lower-risk entry point — it costs less than a third of x2 Cash at 19 Robux. In practice, buy whichever resource is currently blocking your next unlock, and only one of them if your budget is tight.

**Do multiple multiplier tiers stack if I own more than one?**
The catalog does not say. It never states whether tiers stack, replace each other, or require earlier tiers, so treat stacking as unconfirmed and test with a single purchase before buying a second.

**What is the most expensive pass in the catalog?**
In the October 24, 2026 snapshot, SANTA ZONE is listed at 1,999 Robux with no published effect description. It is worth inspecting in game before spending, especially compared with the x6 multipliers at 99 and 119 Robux.
